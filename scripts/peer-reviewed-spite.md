# Peer-Reviewed Spite

30s, 9:16 vertical

Based on pool prompt: *"A cat making deeply offended eye contact while slowly pushing a book off a table, locked medium shot, library light, peer-reviewed spite, book pages fanning in slow-mo with a satisfying thud and smug meow."*

**Logline:** A cat conducts a gravity experiment. The results are personal.

Replace `[CAT]` with one consistent description of your cat and keep it identical across every shot.

## Shot 1 — The Setup (0:00–0:06)

> `[CAT]` sitting upright on a wooden desk beside a thick hardcover book, perfectly still, staring directly into the camera lens with narrowed, judgmental eyes, warm library-style lamp light, bookshelves softly blurred in background, locked-off medium shot at eye level with the cat.

- **Audio:** Quiet, refined classical music (string quartet).
- **Text overlay:** "She's been staring at me for two minutes."

## Shot 2 — First Contact (0:06–0:12)

> `[CAT]` slowly extending one paw and resting it on top of the hardcover book, never breaking eye contact with the camera, head perfectly motionless, only the paw moving, warm lamp light, slow subtle push-in on the locked medium shot.

- **Audio:** String quartet continues. A single low cello note joins.
- **Text overlay:** "No. Don't you dare."

## Shot 3 — The Negotiation (0:12–0:18)

> Extreme close-up of the paw of `[CAT]` sliding the book one inch toward the table edge, then stopping, cut to close-up of the cat's unblinking face staring into camera, deliberate and unhurried, lamp light catching the whiskers, macro detail on the paw pads against the book cover.

- **Audio:** Music stops. Faint wood-scrape sound of the book sliding. Human voice, softly: "Please."
- **Text overlay:** "She heard me beg. It changed nothing."

## Shot 4 — Publication (0:18–0:24)

> Slow-motion shot of the book tipping off the table edge and falling, pages fanning open dramatically mid-air, `[CAT]` visible above still making direct eye contact with the camera, not watching the book at all, warm light, 120fps slow motion, low angle capturing both the falling book and the cat's face.

- **Audio:** Deep slow-mo whoosh, then a huge satisfying THUD with slight room echo.
- **Text overlay:** "FINDINGS: GRAVITY CONFIRMED. AGAIN."

## Shot 5 — Peer Review (0:24–0:30)

> `[CAT]` standing up on the desk, turning, and walking away with tail held high and a slow dignified stride, the fallen book visible on the floor below, warm lamp light, static wide shot as the cat exits frame.

- **Audio:** Smug little meow, then the string quartet resumes triumphantly.
- **Text overlay:** "The study will be repeated tomorrow. Same time."
- **End card:** "Follow for more science."

## Production notes

- Generate the 5 shots as separate clips (6s / 6s / 6s / 6s / 6s) and stitch — most models degrade past ~8s per generation.
- This is the safest of the three to generate cleanly — no fast physical interaction until Shot 4, and even that is a simple object drop.
- Comedy engine: the cat maintains eye contact with the camera through the entire book-pushing sequence, never once looking at the book. That's the joke — sell it in every shot.
