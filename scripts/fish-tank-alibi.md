# The Fish Tank Alibi

30s, 9:16 vertical

Based on pool prompt: *"A cat vigorously patting a goldfish tank for ten straight minutes then acting casual when the human comes in, medium static shot, aquarium glow, criminal with a cover story, water splash taps going quiet instantly and theatrical grooming sounds."*

**Logline:** A crime in progress. A witness approaches. A masterclass in innocence.

Replace `[CAT]` with one consistent description of your cat and keep it identical across every shot.

## Shot 1 — The Crime (0:00–0:07)

> `[CAT]` standing on hind legs against a glowing aquarium, both front paws rapidly patting the glass, a goldfish darting away inside, the room dark except for the blue aquarium glow lighting the cat's intensely focused face, medium static shot.

- **Audio:** Fast rhythmic tap-tap-tap-tap on glass. Tense heist-movie music, low and pulsing.
- **Text overlay:** "3:04 AM. A crime is in progress."

## Shot 2 — Full Commitment (0:07–0:13)

> Close-up on the paws of `[CAT]` drumming against the aquarium glass, the goldfish inside swimming in panicked circles, blue water light rippling across the cat's face, pupils fully dilated, macro shot with shallow focus shifting between paw and fish.

- **Audio:** Tapping intensifies. Heist music builds. Tiny muffled bubble sounds.
- **Text overlay:** "The fish has filed multiple complaints."

## Shot 3 — The Interruption (0:13–0:18)

> The sudden click of a hallway light turning on in the background, `[CAT]` freezing mid-tap with both paws still pressed flat against the aquarium glass, head snapping toward the doorway, eyes enormous, blue glow mixing with warm hallway light, static medium shot.

- **Audio:** All music cuts instantly. Light-switch click. Total silence.
- **Text overlay:** "...witness detected."

## Shot 4 — The Performance (0:18–0:25)

> `[CAT]` smoothly dropping to all fours and immediately sitting down to groom one shoulder with theatrical thoroughness, positioned directly beside the aquarium, eyes half-closed in performed relaxation, the goldfish still hiding behind a plant, mixed blue and warm lighting, slow push-in.

- **Audio:** Loud, exaggerated licking sounds. Innocent whistling melody begins.
- **Text overlay:** "Grooming. She's been grooming this whole time."

## Shot 5 — Case Closed (0:25–0:30)

> Close-up of `[CAT]` mid-groom pausing to look directly into the camera for a long beat, one paw still raised, blue aquarium light flickering, then resuming grooming, locked-off shot.

- **Audio:** Whistling continues. One tiny guilty tap echo in the distance.
- **Text overlay:** "No evidence. No witnesses. No fish were consulted."
- **End card:** "Follow for the trial."

## Production notes

- Generate the 5 shots as separate clips (7s / 6s / 5s / 7s / 5s) and stitch — most models degrade past ~8s per generation.
- The hardest of the four to generate: glass, water refraction, and a live fish are a lot to ask of one shot. If the fish renders badly, keep it hidden behind plants and let the tapping and cat's face sell the joke.
- Comedy engine: the instant, seamless switch from frantic tapping to theatrical innocence the moment the light clicks on. The freeze in Shot 3 should hold just long enough to be uncomfortable before the pivot.
