## Production notes

- Generate the 5 shots as separate clips (6s each) and stitch — most models degrade past ~8s per generation.
- The safest of all four scripts to generate: single continuous action, no precise physical interaction, low risk of AI-artifact glitches.
- Comedy engine: total, uninterrupted serenity in the cat's face regardless of what the vacuum does. The vacuum can bump, spin, or stall — the cat's expression never changes.
